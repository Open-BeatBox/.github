## Ask a question

Use **[Discussions](https://github.com/Open-BeatBox/Open-BeatBox.github.io/discussions)** for anything that is not a defect:

- Build help and "is this normal?"
- Parts sourcing and substitutions
- Designing a behavioural task on the platform
- Showing what you built

Discussions are searchable, so an answer helps the next lab with the same problem. Questions asked privately by email help only one person.

## Report a problem

Open an issue in the repository that owns the material — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for which is which. Include your hardware revision and the guide step or file involved.

## Response times

Open-BEATBox is maintained by a small research team alongside their own experiments. Expect days rather than hours, and longer during teaching terms and conference season. This is not a commercial support channel.
